How the comparisons work

Exports are filtered by origin country; imports by destination country. The field used for each source appears with its chart. Actual weight and chargeable weight are kept separate. A source without the selected weight basis is excluded.

USA WACD annual tables are listed separately. Only years present in the selected physical table are offered. USA CargoIS uses the consolidated table rather than adding overlapping annual copies. Sources remain independent. Null weights are not converted to zero. Row counts are database records, not necessarily shipments.

Screening notes flag changes of at least 40% against the same month last year in kilos or rows. Current partial months are excluded from these change alerts.

Results can be cached for up to 10 minutes. Loading failures are shown explicitly and are never treated as zero or clean data. Older tables may have no recent coverage.
