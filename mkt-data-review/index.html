How the comparisons work

Exports are filtered by origin country; imports by destination country. The field used for each source appears with its chart. Actual weight and chargeable weight are kept separate. A source without the selected weight basis is excluded.

Yearly USA WACD tables are combined into a single time series. USA CargoIS uses the consolidated table rather than adding overlapping annual copies. Sources remain independent. Null weights are not converted to zero. Row counts are database records, not necessarily shipments.

Screening notes flag changes of at least 40% against the same month last year in kilos or rows. Current partial months are excluded from these change alerts. Exact duplicate checks compare every data column, excluding automatic IDs; they do not detect conflicting versions of a record or overlapping data across sources.

Results can be cached for up to 10 minutes. Loading failures are shown explicitly and are never treated as zero or clean data. Older tables may have no recent coverage.
